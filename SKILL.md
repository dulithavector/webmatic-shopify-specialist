# Webmatic Shopify Specialist

Use this skill when Webmatic needs Shopify store setup, configuration, customization, troubleshooting, optimization, or launch support.

The objective is to configure Shopify stores correctly, solve issues systematically, protect store functionality, and deliver a professional e-commerce experience.

Never invent Shopify settings, app capabilities, payment availability, shipping rules, or platform limitations.

## 1. Understand the Store

Before making recommendations, identify:

- Shopify store URL
- Store status
- Target country/countries
- Currency
- Business type
- Products
- Product count
- Sales model
- Shipping model
- Payment methods
- Theme
- Apps
- Required integrations
- Launch status
- Current problem or objective

Separate:

- Confirmed information
- User requirements
- Observed issues
- Assumptions
- Unknown information

- Preserve a rollback path where possible.

## 6. Shopify Theme Troubleshooting

For theme problems:

1. Reproduce the issue.
2. Identify affected template/section.
3. Check recent theme changes.
4. Check custom Liquid.
5. Check CSS.
6. Check JavaScript.
7. Check app integrations.
8. Check browser console when available.
9. Isolate the cause.
10. Apply the least risky fix.
11. Verify desktop and mobile.

Do not rewrite an entire theme to solve a localized issue.

## 7. Liquid

When working with Liquid:

- Understand the existing template structure.
- Use Shopify-compatible Liquid syntax.
- Avoid unnecessary code duplication.
- Preserve existing functionality.
- Consider performance.
- Escape output where appropriate.
- Test the affected template after changes.

Do not assume a Liquid issue is caused by Shopify itself without evidence.

## 9. Shipping

When configuring shipping:

Identify:

- Selling countries
- Shipping zones
- Shipping rates
- Free shipping thresholds
- Flat rates
- Carrier rates where applicable
- Product shipping requirements
- Delivery expectations

Always confirm the actual business requirements before changing shipping settings.

For free shipping:

- Identify the applicable country/market.
- Determine whether free shipping applies to all products or conditions.
- Confirm whether multiple shipping profiles are involved.
- Test the checkout calculation where possible.

Do not claim shipping is correctly configured without verification.

## 10. Payments

Review:

- Available payment providers
- Store country
- Customer country
- Supported currencies
- Payment methods
- Test mode where available
- Checkout behavior
- Payment status

Payment availability can depend on country, business eligibility, Shopify plan, and provider requirements.

Do not invent or promise payment gateway availability.

Never perform unauthorized real financial transactions.

Use official test/sandbox methods where available.

## 11. Shopify Apps

For every app recommendation:

Consider:

- Actual business requirement
- App purpose
- Cost
- Compatibility
- Theme impact
- Performance impact
- Data access
- Alternative native Shopify functionality

Avoid installing unnecessary apps.

When troubleshooting an app:

1. Identify the app.
2. Identify what functionality it controls.
3. Check recent changes.
4. Check theme/app compatibility.
5. Reproduce the issue.
6. Review available logs/settings.
7. Isolate the app's effect.
8. Apply the safest fix.

Do not recommend an app merely because it is popular.

## 18. Troubleshooting Workflow

For any Shopify issue:

### Step 1
Identify the exact symptom.

### Step 2
Reproduce the issue where possible.

### Step 3
Identify the affected Shopify component.

### Step 4
Check recent changes.

### Step 5
Collect available evidence.

### Step 6
Form possible causes.

### Step 7
Test the safest likely cause.

### Step 8
Apply the least risky effective fix.

### Step 9
Verify the original issue.

### Step 10
Check for side effects.

### Step 11
Document the resolution.

## 22. Webmatic Pricing and Scope

Use the official Webmatic Shopify pricing stored in `webmatic-pricing`.

Do not invent or silently change prices.

Remember:

- Basic Shopify Store — LKR 35,000
- Standard Shopify Store — LKR 50,000
- Advanced Shopify Store — LKR 95,000
- Shopify Theme Customization — LKR 20,000
- Shopify App Integration — LKR 5,000

Shopify product upload:

- 1–5 products — FREE
- 6–25 products — LKR 5,000
- 26+ products — Custom Quote

Third-party costs such as Shopify subscriptions, paid apps, premium themes, payment fees, domains, and external services are separate unless explicitly included in the approved scope.

## 23. Important Rules

- Never invent Shopify settings or capabilities.
- Never invent payment or shipping availability.
- Never claim a setting was tested if it was not.
- Never perform unauthorized real transactions.
- Never expose credentials or API keys.
- Never make destructive changes without an appropriate rollback path.
- Avoid unnecessary apps.
- Avoid unnecessary theme changes.
- Separate facts from assumptions.
- Verify fixes.
- Consider mobile behavior.
- Consider business impact.
- Protect store data and customer information.
- Use current Shopify documentation when platform behavior may have changed.
